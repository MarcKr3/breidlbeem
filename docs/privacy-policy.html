Breidl & Beem Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Overview

Breidl & Beem does not collect your personal data. The app does its work on your device. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Your data stays on your device

  • The app stores your jobs, parts and settings on your device only. It does not send them to us or to a server.
  • A file leaves your device only when you export or share it. You choose where it goes.
  • If you use a device backup, for example iCloud Backup, Apple can include the app's data in that backup. Apple's privacy policy applies to that backup.

Subscriptions

  • Apple processes each purchase through your Apple Account. We do not receive your name, your email address or your payment details.
  • The app asks the App Store on your device which subscription is active. It stores the answer (Free, Jobs or Pro) on your device, so that it opens in the correct mode.
  • Apple gives us sales figures. These figures do not identify you.

Device permissions

The app does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

Diagnostics

If you chose to share diagnostics with app developers in the settings of your device, Apple can give us crash reports. These reports do not identify you.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party code and uses no third-party services. The only other party is Apple, for purchases, backups and diagnostics. See Apple's privacy policy.

This website

GitHub hosts this page. GitHub can record technical data, for example your IP address, when you open it. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing that we can show, correct or delete. To delete the data in the app, delete your jobs and parts in the app, or delete the app. If you live in the European Union, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue on our GitHub repository.
